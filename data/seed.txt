The Griffins live at 31 Spooner Street in Quahog, Rhode Island.
Peter Griffin is married to Lois. They have three children: Meg, Chris, and Stewie.
Brian is the family dog. He can talk, and he drinks martinis.
Stewie wants to take over the world and is always plotting against his mother.
Quahog is a fictional town in Rhode Island.
Peter works at the Pawtucket Brewery.
Joe Swanson is a police officer who uses a wheelchair.
Cleveland Brown is Peter's best friend and used to live on Spooner Street.
